title: Practice run

# Opening
Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here today. [pause]

This is a practice script so you can get comfortable reading in the glasses. When you're ready, replace everything in this file with your own words.

# How to write your script
Leave a blank line between paragraphs. One swipe forward jumps to the next paragraph, so keep each one to a thought or two.

Start a line with a hash mark and a space to make a section header, like the blue ones you see here. They're a good place to mark slide changes. [next slide]

Anything in square brackets shows up in gold, like [pause] or [breathe] or [smile], so you know it's a cue and not something to read out loud.

Wrap a word in asterisks to *stress* it. It gets a gold underline.

# Closing
That's everything. Pinch to pause, swipe up or down to change the speed, and swipe back a paragraph if you lose your place. [pause]

Thank you.
